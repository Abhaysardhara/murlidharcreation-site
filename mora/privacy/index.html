This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Mora: Hindi for Kids and Mora: English for Kids (together, the “Apps”), language-learning apps for young children published by Murlidhar Creation (“Murlidhar Creation”, “we”, “us” or “our”). It is written for parents and guardians.

The Apps are designed for children, from about 3 years old, often learning with a parent or carer. Because many of our users are young children, we treat every user as a child and have designed the Apps to collect as little information as possible.

Overview

  • No accountThere is no sign-up or sign-in. We never ask for an email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Everything stays on the deviceYour child’s profile, learning progress and any voice recordings are stored only on your device. We do not receive, upload or have access to them.
  • Child-safe adsThe Apps are free and show ads from Google AdMob that are marked as directed to children, non-personalised, and limited to content rated G. The Apps do not use the advertising ID.
  • Grown-ups only areasSettings, the Parents page, family voice recording and external links are behind a grown-ups check.
  • No sale of dataWe do not sell or share personal information, and we use no analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • No purchases or chatThe Apps have no in-app purchases, no chat and no social features.

1.3 Text-to-speech

Where a recorded voice is not available, the Apps read lesson words and sentences aloud using your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. Only lesson content is spoken this way; names and other personal information are never passed to it. The text-to-speech engine is part of your device and is governed by its provider’s terms.

1.4 Information we do not collect

The Apps do not access the camera, photos, files, contacts, calendar or location, and they do not use the advertising ID. They contain no chat, no social features and no way for a child to communicate with anyone. Lessons, stories and games work offline; an internet connection is used only to show ads.

2. Microphone and voice recordings

The Apps ask for microphone permission for two features. The microphone is on only while the record button is being held down, the screen shows that recording is in progress, and recording stops if the App goes into the background.

  • Speaking practice. Your child can say a word or read a line aloud and hear their own voice played back. This recording is kept only temporarily on the device and is replaced by the next one.
  • Family voice (“Dadi’s Voice” in the Hindi app, “Grandma’s Voice” in the English app). Behind the grown-ups check, a family member can record letters, words and cheers, which the App then plays in lessons instead of its own voice. These recordings are saved on the device until you delete them in the App or uninstall it.

Recordings are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else. If you do not allow microphone access, every other part of the Apps still works.

3. How information is used

Information stored on your device is used only by the App on your device, to provide lessons, remember progress, show the Parents page and play recorded voices.

Information collected by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK is used only to serve and measure non-personalised ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and detect and prevent fraud. It is not used for interest-based advertising, profiling or remarketing.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar laws apply, the information on your device is processed to provide the service you asked for, and the limited advertising processing relies on our legitimate interest in funding free educational apps with contextual, non-personalised ads, with the protections for children described in this policy.

4. Advertising

4.1 How ads are configured

Every ad request from the Apps is:

  • tagged as directed to children (tagForChildDirectedTreatment), so Google treats it under its policies for children, including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA);
  • tagged as from a user under the age of consent (tagForUnderAgeOfConsent), for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland;
  • non-personalised only: no interest-based ads, no remarketing and no profiling;
  • limited to ads rated G (suitable for all audiences) under Google’s maximum ad content rating.

4.2 Where ads appear

  • Interstitial ads, full-screen ads shown occasionally at a natural break: after finishing an island, or after some lessons. Ads never appear inside lessons, stories or games.
  • A banner ad on the Parents page only, which is behind the grown-ups check.
  • Rewarded videos, which play only when chosen, to earn extra stars or outfits for Mor, with a daily limit. Watching them is always optional.

5. Notifications

A parent can turn on one daily reminder from the Parents page. It is scheduled on the device itself, at the time you choose; we do not run a notification server and receive no information about it. You can turn it off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information about you or your child.

On the family voice page, behind the grown-ups check, a parent can choose to send an invitation message to a relative using the device’s own sharing menu. The message goes only to the person and app you choose, and we do not see it.

The only third party that receives information through the Apps is Google LLC, as our advertising provider, as described in section 1.2.

8. Children’s safety

  • The Parents page, family voice recording, settings and all external links (such as this policy and our support email) are behind a grown-ups check, so children cannot change settings, record over the App’s voice or leave the App.
  • The Apps contain no chat, no social features, no user-generated content shared with others and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The information described in this policy is either kept on the device or, for ads, limited to what Google collects under its child-directed settings.

9. Retention and deletion

Information stored by the Apps remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • delete a child’s profile and progress on the Parents page;
  • delete individual family voice recordings on the family voice page;
  • clear the App’s data in Settings → Apps → [App name] → Storage → Clear storage; or
  • uninstall the App, which removes everything it stored on the device.

We do not keep copies of any of this information, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Information collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policies.

10. Security

Because the Apps do not send your child’s information to us, it is not exposed on any server of ours. Information on the device is protected by Android’s app sandboxing and your device’s screen lock. Ad requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we design the Apps to keep the information they handle to a minimum.

11. International transfers

Google may process advertising information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Google states that it relies on appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses; see the Google privacy frameworks documentation.

12. Parents’ rights

Parents and guardians have rights over their children’s information. Because we do not collect or store information that identifies you or your child, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App, as described in section 9.

12.1 United States (COPPA)

We do not collect personal information from children as defined by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. Ad requests are tagged as child-directed so that Google applies its COPPA protections. If we ever wanted to collect personal information from children, we would first give notice to parents and obtain verifiable parental consent. Parents may contact us at any time with questions or requests about their child’s information.

12.2 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, to object to processing, and to data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Ad requests are tagged as from users under the age of consent, so no consent form is shown and ads are non-personalised.

12.3 India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the processing of children’s personal data requires verifiable parental consent, and tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children are not permitted. The Apps do not collect children’s personal data on our side, and ads are non-personalised and child-directed.

12.4 Making a request

To ask a question or make a request, contact us using the details in section 14. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period required by applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we change the Apps or when laws change. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and publish the updated policy before the change takes effect in the Apps. If we ever wanted to collect personal information from children, we would ask for a parent’s consent first.
