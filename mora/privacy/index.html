Privacy Policy

For Mora: Hindi for Kids and Mora: English for Kids (the “apps”)
Effective date: 11 November 2026

In short

  • The apps teach Hindi and English to beginners of any age. Because many learners are young children (from about 3 years old), often learning with a parent or carer, we designed them to be safe for children and treat every user as a child.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for an email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Your child’s name, learning progress and voice recordings are stored only on your device. We do not receive, see or upload them.
  • The apps are free and show ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalised or interest-based ads, family-friendly content only.
  • We do not sell any data, and we use no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

1. Who we are

The apps are made by Murlidhar Creation (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy or your child’s privacy, write to us at support@murlidharcreation.net.

2. Information kept on your device only

To work, the apps save the following on your phone or tablet. It never leaves the device through us, and we cannot access it.

  • Child profiles: the name (or nickname) and animal picture you choose for each child. Use a first name or nickname; a full name is not needed.
  • Learning progress: lessons finished, stars, stickers, quiz answers, minutes played each day and game scores, so the app can continue where your child left off and show it to you on the Parents page.
  • Settings: for example the time of the daily reminder, if you turn it on.
  • Voice recordings: see section 3.

3. Microphone and voice recordings

The apps ask for microphone permission for two features. The microphone is only on while the record button is being held down, the screen shows that it is recording, and recording stops if the app goes into the background.

  • Speaking practice: your child can say a word or read a line aloud and hear their own voice played back. This recording is kept only temporarily on the device and is replaced by the next one.
  • Family voice (“Dadi’s Voice” in the Hindi app, “Grandma’s Voice” in the English app): behind a grown-ups check, a family member can record letters, words and cheers, which the app then plays in lessons instead of its own voice. These recordings are saved on the device until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

Recordings are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else. If you do not allow the microphone, everything else in the apps still works.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The apps are free because they show ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google. Ads appear only in a few places: after finishing an island or some lessons, a banner on the grown-ups Parents page, and optional reward videos that unlock stars. There are no ads inside lessons, stories or games.

Every ad request is set up for children:

  • marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, so Google treats it under its rules for children (including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA);
  • non-personalised only: no interest-based ads and no remarketing;
  • limited to ads rated G (suitable for all audiences);
  • the apps do not access the device’s advertising ID.

To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often an ad is shown, Google’s ad software in the app may automatically collect limited technical information, such as the IP address, device type and operating system, app information, and how ads are viewed and tapped. We do not receive information that identifies you or your child. Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Notifications

A parent can turn on one daily reminder from the Parents page. It is created on the device itself, at the time you choose. We do not run a notification server, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

6. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any information about you or your child. On the Family voice page, a grown-up can choose to send an invitation message to a relative using the phone’s own sharing menu. That message goes only to the person and app you pick; we do not see it.

7. Keeping children safe

  • The Parents and Family voice pages are behind a grown-ups check, so children cannot change settings, record over the app’s voice or leave the app through links there.
  • The apps contain no chat, no social features, no in-app purchases and no links that a child can follow out of the app.

8. Deleting information

  • Delete a child’s profile and progress on the Parents page (behind the grown-ups check).
  • Delete individual family recordings on the Family voice page.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Because we do not hold any of this information ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For information collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s privacy policy linked above.

9. Your rights and questions

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as asking what is held about you or your child, or asking for it to be deleted. As described above, we do not collect or store personal information about you or your child. If you have any question or request, contact us at support@murlidharcreation.net and we will reply as soon as we can.

10. Changes to this policy

If the apps change in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the apps. If we ever wanted to collect personal information from children, we would ask for a parent’s consent first.
