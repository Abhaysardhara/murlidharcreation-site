Privacy Policy

For Gummy Fling (the “game”)
Effective date: 14 November 2026

In short

  • Gummy Fling is a physics puzzle game for players of all ages.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Your progress, coins and items are stored only on your device. We do not receive, see or upload them.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, set up as family-friendly: non-personalised ads only, suitable-for-everyone content.
  • We do not sell any data, and we use no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

1. Who we are

Gummy Fling is made by Murlidhar Creation (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, write to us at support@murlidharcreation.net.

2. Information kept on your device only

To work, the game saves the following on your phone or tablet. It never leaves the device through us, and we cannot access it.

  • Progress: levels finished, stars and best scores, so the game can continue where you left off.
  • Gold coins and items: your coin balance, the gummies, power upgrades and outfits you have unlocked with coins, and the gummy and outfit you have chosen.
  • Settings and reminders of what you have seen: sound on or off, which how-to and new-obstacle cards you have already seen, and the dates of your daily gift and free coin bags.

The game does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos, and it does not send notifications. Levels are built on your device, so the game itself does not need the internet; the internet is used only to show ads.

3. Gold coins

Gold coins are earned only by playing, from the daily gift, and by choosing to watch an optional ad. They can be spent only inside the game, have no real-world value, and cannot be bought with money. There are no in-app purchases.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google. Ads appear only in a few places: sometimes between levels, a banner on the level map, and optional reward videos that you choose to watch to double your coins, get free coins or get extra shots. There are no ads during a shot.

Every ad request is set up to be safe for all ages:

  • marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, so Google treats it under its rules for children (including the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA);
  • non-personalised only: no interest-based ads and no remarketing;
  • limited to ads rated G (suitable for all audiences).

To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often an ad is shown, Google’s ad software in the game may automatically collect limited technical information, such as the IP address, device type and operating system, app information, a device or advertising identifier where the law and your device settings allow it, and how ads are viewed and tapped. We do not receive information that identifies you. Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

On Android you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any information about you. The only other party involved is Google, which serves the ads as described in section 4.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It contains no chat, no social features, no in-app purchases and no way to contact other players, and its ads are set up under Google’s rules for children as described in section 4.

7. Deleting information

  • Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on the device.
  • On Android you can also clear the game’s data in Settings → Apps → Gummy Fling → Storage → Clear storage.

Because we do not hold any of this information ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For information collected by Google for advertising, see Google’s privacy policy linked above.

8. Your rights and questions

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as asking what is held about you or asking for it to be deleted. As described above, we do not collect or store personal information about you. If you have any question or request, contact us at support@murlidharcreation.net and we will reply as soon as we can.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the game.
