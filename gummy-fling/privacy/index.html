This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Gummy Fling (the “App”), a physics puzzle game for Android published by Murlidhar Creation (“Murlidhar Creation”, “we”, “us” or “our”). It covers what the App stores on your device, what our advertising provider collects, why, and the choices and rights you have.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the App.

Overview

  • No accountThe App has no sign-up or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details.
  • Your game stays on your deviceProgress, coins, unlocked items and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive, upload or have access to them.
  • Ads by Google AdMobThe App is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google collects certain device information to serve and measure ads.
  • Your choicesWhere required by law, including in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the App.
  • No sale of dataWe do not sell your personal information, and we use no analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • No purchasesThe App has no in-app purchases. Gold coins are earned in play and have no monetary value.

Google processes this information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. For more detail, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

1.3 Information we do not collect

The App does not access your camera, microphone, photos, files, contacts, calendar or precise location. It does not send notifications, and it has no chat, social features or way to communicate with other players. Levels are generated on your device; an internet connection is used only to request and show ads.

2. How information is used

Information stored on your device is used only by the App on your device, to provide the game and remember your progress and preferences.

Information collected by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK is used to deliver, measure and improve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, show ads based on your interests.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar laws apply, the legal bases for processing are: consent, for personalised advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes; and legitimate interests, for serving non-personalised ads that fund the free App, and for fraud prevention and security. You can withdraw your consent at any time, as described in section 3.

3. Advertising

3.1 Where ads appear

  • Interstitial ads, which are full-screen ads shown occasionally when you leave a level you have completed. They are never shown during play, after a lost level, or within the first levels of the game.
  • Banner ads on the main menu and the level map, never over the playing area.
  • Rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward: a hint, extra shots, doubled coins, a free coin bag or a doubled daily gift. Watching them is always optional.

3.2 Ad content

The App requests ads rated PG or below under Google’s maximum ad content rating, so that ads are suitable for a general audience.

3.3 Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). When you first open the App, Google’s consent form asks for your choice about personalised ads and related data processing. You can review or change your choice at any time using Settings → Privacy settings in the App.
  • Device settings (Android). You can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, under Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Google → Ads). If you delete your advertising ID, apps can no longer use it for personalised ads.
  • Google ad settings. You can manage how Google personalises ads at adssettings.google.com.

If you opt out of personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will be based on general context rather than your interests.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share information stored on your device with anyone, because it never leaves your device through us.

The only third party that receives information through the App is Google LLC, as our advertising provider, as described in section 1.2. Google may share information with advertising partners in accordance with its own policies and your consent choices. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, but we hold no personal information about you to disclose.

6. Retention and deletion

Information stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • uninstalling the App, which removes all of its data from your device; or
  • clearing the App’s data in Settings → Apps → Gummy Fling → Storage → Clear storage, which resets your progress, coins and items.

We do not keep copies of this information on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Information collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policies; see How Google retains data it collects.

7. Security

Because the App does not transmit your game data to us, it is not exposed to risks on our servers. Ad requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit. Information stored on your device is protected by your device’s own security measures, such as your screen lock and Android’s app sandboxing. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we design the App to keep the information it handles to a minimum.

8. International transfers

Google may process advertising information in countries other than the one you live in, including the United States. Google states that it relies on appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and recognised data privacy frameworks. More information is available in the Google privacy frameworks documentation.

9. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and the App has no chat, social features, purchases or links that let players contact each other.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at the address below, and we will help address the request.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. Because we do not collect or store personal information that identifies you, most requests can be met directly on your device or through Google, as explained below.

10.1 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data; to object to processing based on legitimate interests; to data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before you withdrew it. You can withdraw consent for personalised advertising at any time in Settings → Privacy settings in the App. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10.2 United States

Residents of certain U.S. states, including California, may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, to correct it, and to opt out of the sale of personal information or its “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information. The use of your advertising ID by Google for personalised ads may be considered “sharing” under some state laws; you can opt out at any time by deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ads personalisation in your device settings, as described in section 3.3. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

10.3 Making a request

To make a request or ask a question about your rights, contact us using the details in section 12. For information held by Google, you can also use the tools described in the Google Privacy Policy.
